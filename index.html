About Teddy Candy

Bringing joy to the table, one crave-worthy bite at a time.

Teddy Candy is a Washington, DC home-based food business built around flavor, personality, and pure fun. We combine sweet treats, juicy fruit, savory comfort food, and eye-catching desserts into a menu that feels fresh, exciting, and made for sharing.

Whether you’re craving a refreshing specialty drink, a juicy candy fruit snack, a hot meal from the grill, or a dessert that steals the whole spotlight, Teddy Candy is here to keep your taste buds happy and your cravings satisfied.
